Breaking News May 5, 2026

American Athletes Sweep Women's Spots at Copa Sur, Sparking Regional Controversy

American athletes Anikha Greer and Miley Wade swept both CrossFit Games qualifying spots at the Copa Sur Semifinal in Brazil this past weekend, sparking intense backlash from South American fans who argue the regional competition should prioritize local athletes. The controversy stems from CrossFit's decision to remove the South American citizenship requirement that existed in 2025, allowing North American athletes to compete for the limited Games tickets in Brazil. With only two women's spots available and both going to Americans, South American women were completely shut out of Games qualification from their own regional Semifinal.

The Instagram announcement generated hundreds of critical comments from frustrated fans defending regional representation. User @ramirodramis wrote: "Why do the gringos now go down to the South Copa to steal South American places? When you think CrossFit can't get any worse you keep getting over it," receiving 212 likes. User @dannyta_cm expressed deeper concern about representation, stating: "The South Cup should not have received athletes from other regions, in the case of women we were left without female representatives! Who do we support as Latinos if they come to remove the dues? What about representation and what is crossfit for everyone? It's totally unfair." User @mirnajade added sarcastically: "Nice idea adding Americans to Copa Sur... except now the true American athletes (from Colombia, Brazil, Chile, Argentina, Guatemala, Cuba, Republica Domenicana, etc.) aren't even making it to the Games. Super democratic lol. That's why Hyrox is gonna destroy you guys in a couple of years," garnering 173 likes.

The debate highlights broader tensions about fairness in CrossFit's qualification system, with many commenters calling for either regional restrictions or increased quota allocations to ensure South American representation at the Games. While some defended the open format by arguing the Games should feature the "fittest on Earth" regardless of geography, the dominant sentiment expressed frustration that a South American Semifinal produced zero female South American qualifiers. Whether this backlash will be enough to sway CrossFit into region locking Semifinals for 2027 remains uncertain. Will they again leave the decision to individual event organizers?

Breaking News May 3, 2026

Copa Sur Sends Four Elite Athletes to the 2026 CrossFit Games

Anikha Greer and Miley Wade punched their tickets to the CrossFit Games in the women's division, while Kaylan Souza and Benjamin Reyes claimed the two men's qualifying spots. With only two Games spots available per division at Copa Sur, the final standings left no room for error across the six event weekend.

Greer makes her second trip back to the Games, while Wade, a three time teenage CrossFit Games athlete, qualifies as an elite individual for the first time. On the men's side, Souza returns to the Games for his fourth appearance, and Reyes, also a teen Games athlete, makes his first elite individual trip to the CrossFit Games.

Those four athletes now advance to the 2026 CrossFit Games, scheduled for July 24th-26th at SAP Center in San Jose, California where they will be competing for the title of Fittest on Earth.

Henrik Haapalainen Claims Lone Far East Games Spot

Henrik Haapalainen secured the lone men’s CrossFit Games ticket at the Far East Throwdown in Busan, Korea, after a dramatic final event shakeup on the leaderboard. Sven entered the last workout in the overall lead, poised for his first individual Games appearance, but an unfortunate finish in the finale opened the door for Haapalainen to move ahead and claim the top spot.

With only one Games spot available for both the men and women at the Far East Throwdown, every point mattered, and Haapalainen’s closing performance was enough to send him back to the CrossFit Games for the first time since 2024, while Sven, still searching for his first individual Games trip, was left just outside qualification.

Breaking News May 2, 2026

CrossFit Hendersonville Games Ticket Punched

CrossFit Hendersonville a Mayhem Athlete team has become the first team to secure their spot at the 2026 CrossFit Games, dominating the competition at Copa Sur in São José, Brazil. The team, comprised of Dalton Rodgers, Lindsey Lane, Kensie Campbell, and Wes Peters, delivered commanding performances all weekend of the South American Semifinal.

The Hendersonville squad wasted no time establishing themselves as the team to beat, winning both Event 1 and Event 2 and then never placing lower than 4th on the remaining three events . They were leading going into the final and finished that workout in second place to secure the overall win for the weekend. Copa Sur 2026 offers only one team qualifying spot to the CrossFit Games, making the competition among the 30 elite teams particularly fierce. By securing the top position early in the season, CrossFit Hendersonville has positioned themselves comfortably for the next several months to soley focus on training for the CrossFit Games.

There are still 19 spots yet to be claimed for the CrossFit Games in the team division, who will be next?

Breaking News April 27, 2026

Breaking News CrossFit New CEO Announced

Bruce Edwards was announced this morning in an all hands on deck meeting at CrossFit HQ as the company’s new CEO. He previously served as CrossFit’s Chief Operating Officer from October 2013 to September 2019, a period when the company scaled to more than 16,000 affiliates worldwide and saw explosive growth in both its training business and the CrossFit Games. Since leaving CrossFit, Edwards has worked across the fitness industry, including a brief stint as Chief Growth Officer at In Shape Health Clubs, then COO and later President of barre3, and he is currently the CEO of Core Development and Management, a major Planet Fitness franchise group. How the community responds to his return and corporate style background will go a long way in determining whether this next chapter for CrossFit feels like a return to its growth years, a shift toward a broader mainstream fitness play, or something new that can successfully blend both.

Sevan Matossian and Matthew Souza will be going live shortly to talk about the newly appointed CEO news, so tune in to the CF Network YouTube channel for live reaction and analysis.

Legends Del Mar Final Leaderboard And Games Tickets

Legends Del Mar closed with a brutal chipper final 60 toes to bar, 50 wall balls, 40 box jump overs, 30 double dumbbell devil press and a front rack lunge to the finish, with the mens dumbbell weight quietly changed from 80s to 60s the night before and adding fuel to already tight points races. News Story April 26, 2026

One Event Left And Two Tickets On The Line At Del Mar

Olivia Kerstetter has done her job. With 385 points and a dominant string of finishes, she has effectively locked up the top spot and the first womens ticket to the CrossFit Games, leaving all the tension focused on the race for second. Abigail Domit 330, Lydia Fish 325, and Kyra Milligan 315 are separated by just 15 points with one event remaining, and only one of them will join Kerstetter at the Games.

Domit currently holds that second qualifying position on the back of steady, top 6 finishes and a huge Event 3 performance that pushed her into control of her own destiny. Fish sits only five points back, close enough that a single placing in the final could flip their spots if she can get ahead of Domit when it counts. Milligan, armed with an event win and big event upside, needs another punch out performance in the finale and a misstep from one or both women ahead of her to turn her outside lane into a last minute ticket.

On the mens side, Dallin Pepper has been nearly untouchable. With 390 points, two first place finsishes and two second place finshes, he has all but secured the mens title and his return trip to the Games, shifting the spotlight to the men behind him. James Sprague 360 holds second and the final Games ticket for now, but Dylan Hamming 350 is just 10 points back, and Tudor Magda 330 is still within striking distance if chaos hits in the final test.

Spragues weekend started with a statement Event 1 win, and he has held his ground through consistent top 4 finishes, but Hamming has been closing the gap with his own run of podium level results. For Hamming, the path is clear, he needs to beat Sprague by multiple spots in the finale and let the field do the rest. Magda needs his best finish of the weekend plus stumbles from both men ahead of him, but in a 100 point final where one bad heat can mean a 20 to 30 point swing, the last event will decide who will join Dallin & punch thier ticket to the CrossFit Games.

Competition Update April 25, 2026

Day One Complete at Legends Del Mar

Day one is done for the Legends Del Mar semifinal. Event 1 featured a waterfall style start for the Age group athletes and that made tracking the field a bit challenging. Event one was a non traditional workout testing muscular stamina over endurance capacity, the athletes were required to all have the Stepr bike and Ski dampers at 10, some athletes choosing to stand but most realizing that was the best way to approach that test.

Event 2 brought quick, explosive action to close the day. Kyra Milligan and Dallin Pepper each capturing event wins. Pepper leads the Elite Men with 195 total points after placing second in Event 1 and winning Event 2.

After two events, Dallin Pepper leads the Elite Men division with 195 total points, ahead of James Sprague with 185 points. Olivia Kerstetter tops the Elite Women leaderboard with 195 points after winning Event 1 and placing second in Event 2, while Emily Rolfe sits in second with 180 points. Competition continues through Sunday as athletes battle for qualifying spots to the 2026 CrossFit Games.
