Rogue Invitational Day One: Mertens and Migała Lead as Horvath and Adler Sit 12th

Day one of the 2026 Rogue Invitational in Aberdeen, Scotland, is in the books, and it delivered surprises from the very first event. Colten Mertens leads the men with 270 points, and Gabriela Migała leads the women with 250. Both defending champions, Jeffrey Adler and Laura Horvath, ended the day in 12th place.

Event 1: River Dee
The opener was seven rounds of 11 calorie ski, 7 wall walks, 11 calorie ski and 7 devil's presses. James Sprague won the men's heat in 18:29.07, with Dallin Pepper second and Harry Lightfoot third. Lucy Campbell took the women's win in 18:50.15, ahead of Siria Meha and Migała.

The biggest story of the event was at the other end of the leaderboard. Horvath, the previous champion, finished dead last in 20th in 23:51.52 and scored zero points. Ricky Garard also had a bad start, placing 17th in 22:38.06. Fans in the livestream chat said Garard was hit with multiple no reps on the wall walks, though that was not shown on the broadcast.

Event 2: Single Malt
Single Malt was a descending ladder of heavy back squats with sprints in between. The men went 10, 8, 6, 4 and 2 reps from 315 up to 405 pounds, and the women climbed from 225 to 285. Mertens came in favored to win the second and third events, and he did just that. He made easy work of the back squats and won in 4:45.97, more than 17 seconds ahead of Bayley Martin in second. Jay Crouch was third.

Horvath bounced right back. One event after finishing last, she won the women's heat in 5:35.37, ahead of Kyra Milligan and Campbell. Danielle Brandon took a different approach. Because there was no minimum work requirement, she chose not to complete any reps on Event 2 and take a zero. She was scored at the cap plus 40, last in the event.

Event 3: The Viking
Day one closed with 60 seconds of max reps on the Viking press. Mertens went 23 reps unbroken, then 7 more. On the broadcast it appeared he locked out an eighth rep of that set, which would have been his 31st press, right at the buzzer, but it was not counted. He still won the event with 30 reps, which put him in the lead overall for day one. Garard tied Mertens at 30 reps but was second on the tiebreak, a strong finish to a tough day. Tudor Magda was third with 29.

Dani Speegle won the women's event with an impressive 33 reps, going 7, 7, 6, 4, 4 and 5 all in the 60 seconds. Alex Gazan was second with 29, and Aimee Cringle was third, also with 29. Horvath finished outside the top 10 in 11th and ended her day in 12th overall.

Appeal Ruling on Event 3
There were some appeals on Event 3, including one about James Sprague and Bayley Martin. The two men tied at 23 reps. The tiebreak was most reps completed by the 30 second mark, and they tied that too with 15 each. Both athletes were awarded the same points and placement, tied for 10th with 55 points each.

Women: Migała Puts Her Eggs in One Basket
Migała had the best rounded day of any woman, finishing third, fourth and sixth to take the lead. Her return to competition comes after she qualified for the CrossFit Games but decided to sit it out to focus on training for the Rogue Invitational instead. She has put all her eggs in this basket, and so far she looks very consistent.

Campbell is not going down without a fight. She sits second with 220 points after winning Event 1 and taking third on Event 2. There are also many athletes within striking distance of the top five, which could make it difficult. Gazan is third with 210, and Milligan, Olivia Kerstetter and Emma Tall are tied on 205. Milligan had a solid second and third event, placing second in Single Malt and ninth on the Viking press.

Paige Rodgers had trouble in Events 1 and 2, placing 18th and 19th. There are reports that Rodgers has been battling the flu and diarrhea, which would explain the slow start. She did very well on Event 3, though, with 24 reps for eighth. She is 17th overall.

Men: Mertens on Top, Adler in 12th
Mertens leads Pepper by just five points, 270 to 265, with Sprague third on 235. Crouch also had a solid second and third event, taking third in Single Malt and eighth on the Viking press. That puts him fourth overall with 220 points, just ahead of Magda on 210.

Returning champion Adler had a rough day one and sits in 12th with 155 points. He was sixth in River Dee and eighth in Single Malt, but managed just 13 reps on the Viking press for 18th. On the men's side you cannot rule out the Brute men or the other PRVN men either.

Coaching Shakeup Rumors
Away from the floor, rumors are circulating that Danielle Brandon has left coach Adam Neiffer and is now working with Domenic D'Agostino, better known as Coach Dom. Brandon started working with Neiffer and Jesse Bifano ahead of the 2025 CrossFit Games, and Neiffer coached her Team Outcast at Battle of the Training Camps alongside Tudor Magda. Magda is now rumored to be working with Neiffer himself.

James Sprague hinted at both moves on CF Network News. "Someone just switched to that coach though (we think Tudor). That's also a big deal. That that other person left this coach (we know Danielle Brandon left Neiffer)." None of the athletes or coaches have confirmed the changes.

A Fitting Farewell to Aberdeen
The venue at P&J Live looks amazing. The turf and the hill on the competition floor are a great touch for the third and final year of the Rogue Invitational in Aberdeen. Rogue owner Bill Henniger and the city have put on a spectacular event for the athletes and the fans. There is still no word on where the Rogue Invitational will be held next year.

What Comes Next
There are still two days left and six more events, so it is not impossible for Adler and Horvath to climb back up. Saturday brings Packout, The Duel VI and Orlando, and Sunday closes with Pulling Power, Dunnottar and the Highland Sprint. It is sure to be an exciting weekend.

CrossFit Releases 2027 Season Guide With New Teen Division, Team Qualifiers and National Championships

CrossFit has released its full guide to competing in 2027, and the new season brings some of the biggest structural changes in years. There is a new 18 to 19 teen division, teams are skipping Quarterfinals for in person qualifiers, and a new National Championships track is launching. One thing missing is the pairs division for the Open that Dave Castro mentioned earlier this fall.

The Open Moves to Wednesdays
Registration opens January 7, 2027. The Open stays at three weeks, but workouts will now drop on Wednesdays instead of Thursdays. 27.1 runs February 17 to 22, 27.2 runs February 24 to March 1, and 27.3 runs March 3 to 8. Athletes have until Monday at 5 p.m. PT to submit scores. CrossFit says new Open features will be revealed Wednesday, October 28, after the Affiliate Town Hall.

Individual Path to the Games
The top 25 percent of individuals advance from the Open to Quarterfinals, held online March 15 to 22. From there, the top 2,000 men and 2,000 women move on to Semifinals. In person Semifinals run April through June, followed by the Online Semifinals June 14 to 21 as a last chance to qualify.

Two rules stand out. Athletes can accept invites to only two in person Semifinal events, though they may still compete at the Online Semifinals. The top three men and top three women from each Semifinal, including the online event, earn a spot at the Games. The field at the 2027 CrossFit Games will be 40 men and 40 women. The location and dates have not been announced.

Teams Go Straight to In Person Qualifiers
Teams will skip Quarterfinals entirely. After the Open, teams advance to the new Team Qualifiers, a series of in person events hosted by select partners from April through June. Each event may have its own qualification process, but all workouts will be standardized across every Team Qualifier and teams will be ranked on one global leaderboard. The number of teams that qualify for the Games has not been released.

New 18 to 19 Teen Division
The 18 to 19 age group is now official, joining the 14 to 15 and 16 to 17 divisions. Each teen division sends 300 athletes to Semifinals. Teens compete only at the Online Semifinals, June 24 to 28, to qualify for the Teenage CrossFit Games hosted by Pit Prodigies. Masters can earn spots at in person Semifinals from April through June or at the Online Semifinals in June, with the Masters CrossFit Games run by Magic City Games.

Masters Games Head to Birmingham
CrossFit's guide lists the location for every 2027 Games as coming soon, but Magic City Games, led by director Micah Shoemaker, has already announced where the masters will compete. The 2027 Masters CrossFit Games will be held September 18 to 19, 2027, at Bill Harris Arena in Birmingham, Alabama.

That confirms the age group and adaptive championships will be separate events from the individual and team CrossFit Games. The Masters Games will be run by Magic City Games, the Teenage Games by Pit Prodigies and the Adaptive Games by WheelWOD. The location of the main CrossFit Games is still unannounced, but reports surfaced in September that CrossFit sent part of its Games team to scout venues in Spain, France and Germany. Dave Castro has said he would like the 21st year of the Games, which falls in 2027, to be held in Europe. Nothing has been confirmed.

Adaptive Changes
The Adaptive Open, run by WheelWOD, follows the same schedule as the CrossFit Open. The top 20 athletes in each division advance to the Adaptive Online Semifinals, May 13 to 18. New this year, Games spots for each division and each sex will be based on Open registrations.

National Championships
CrossFit is adding National Championships to its licensed event program, running alongside the Games season. Athletes go from the Open to Quarterfinals to a country specific National Championship event between April and December, and the winner in each host country is crowned National Champion. If a country does not have a host event, the top athletes from the Open or Quarterfinals earn the title.

No Word on Pairs
The guide makes no mention of the Open pairs division Castro discussed on his Week in Review in September. Pairs was also not mentioned on the Sport of Fitness Podcast episode breaking down the 2027 season. It is possible pairs is one of the new Open features set for the October 28 reveal, but CrossFit has not said.

Bruce Edwards Announces Two New CrossFit Partnerships at AffiliateCon

CrossFit CEO Bruce Edwards used his time on stage at AffiliateCon 2026 to announce two new partnerships aimed at helping affiliate owners. One is with Best Hour of Their Day, the business mentorship company that hosts AffiliateCon. The other is with Healthy Steps Nutrition, better known as HSN. The conference ran October 16 to 18 at the Omni Las Colinas in Dallas.

CrossFit and HSN
HSN founder Nicole Aucoin shared the moment on Instagram after Edwards joined her on stage. "16 years ago I stepped foot inside a CrossFit affiliate for the first time. Today, the CEO of CrossFit joined me on the stage at AffiliateCon to share what we've been working on together," she wrote.

"After 10+ years of working with CrossFit affiliate owners one at a time, today we took a big step forward to serve the community together," Aucoin added. She thanked the owners and coaches who have trusted HSN to support their members' health "during the other 23 hours outside the gym."

HSN is not new to CrossFit. Aucoin is a registered dietitian and the founder of Healthy Steps Nutrition, CrossFit HSN and HSN Mentoring. HSN Mentoring joined the CrossFit Affiliate Partner Network in 2022 and helps gyms build habit based nutrition coaching programs. The two sides also introduced a CrossFit x HSN Ambassador Program at the 2026 CrossFit Games.

CrossFit and Best Hour of Their Day
Best Hour of Their Day was founded by longtime affiliate owners and CrossFit Level 4 trainers Jason Ackerman and Jason Fernandez. Its Affiliate University program is already promoted by CrossFit as the first and only CrossFit endorsed business coaching program for affiliates. Best Hour also sponsored the 2026 CrossFit Owners and Coaches Conference, where Edwards sat down with Fernandez in July to talk about rebuilding the relationship between HQ and affiliates.

What Comes Next
Neither CrossFit nor its partners have released the full terms of the new agreements. CF Network News will share more details as they are announced.

Roman Khrennikov Withdraws From Rogue and Announces Move to Madison

Roman Khrennikov will not compete at the 2026 Rogue Invitational, and he is about to have a new home. One night after news of his late withdrawal broke, Khrennikov told the Out of the Cage podcast that he is moving to Madison, Wisconsin.

A Late Withdrawal
The Barbell Spin reported Thursday that Khrennikov has pulled out of the Rogue Invitational, which runs October 23 to 25 at P&J Live in Aberdeen, Scotland. "I don't want to show up unprepared," Khrennikov said.

The injury dates back to the World Fitness Project event the last weekend of August. Khrennikov hurt his left leg during the 40 yard sprint, and his right leg cramped during the 10k run the next morning. He withdrew from that competition and did not think the injury was serious at the time.

Khrennikov is the fifth man to withdraw from the Rogue field. Pat Vellner, Austin Hatfield, Jonne Koski and Justin Medeiros have also pulled out.

The Call
On Thursday night's episode of Out of the Cage, Ricky called Khrennikov live on the show and asked if he had missed his flight. Khrennikov said he had cancelled it just a couple of hours earlier. Then he shared bigger news. "I'm just moving to Wisconsin, Madison," he said.

Khrennikov said the move could happen as soon as Saturday and that he is packing everything now. He is leaving Cookeville, Tennessee, and said the biggest reason is his son. "It's so good for my son, for education," he said. "I want to stay in the city."

Madison is a familiar place for the CrossFit world. It hosted the CrossFit Games every year from 2017 through 2023, except 2020 because of COVID.

Claire Bays Hits Day 442 of Her Daily Mile

Claire Bays has run a mile every day for 442 straight days. In a new interview clip posted by the Best Hour of Their Day, the CrossFit Level 3 Trainer and WOD Prep podcast host explained how the streak started and why she has not stopped.

How It Started
Bays said the mile came from not liking the way she felt during metcons. She wanted some momentum, and a daily mile was an easy place to start. It also became a way to bring people together. "CrossFit can be intimidating for many, right? But a mile is not so intimidating," she said.

She ran online challenges called 90 and 90, meaning 90 miles in 90 days. Another affiliate owner who joined while coming out of a tough season of his life went on to lead a group of his own. Bays said the goal was to connect those groups online and then get some of them into an affiliate.

The Gratitude Mile
The gratitude part came later. Bays now posts her mile on social media every day along with something she is grateful for. She will lead a Gratitude Mile run on Saturday morning at AffiliateCon 2026, held October 16 to 18 at the Omni Las Colinas in Dallas. Attendees can RSVP in the AffiliateCon app.

Crash Crucible Check In: Drug Testing, New Tie Break, and a Farmers Carry Twist

SPARTANBURG, S.C. Athletes checked in today at CrossFit Crash ahead of the 2026 Crash Crucible, and event organizer and programmer J.R. Howell used the athlete briefing to deliver a handful of announcements. The biggest headline: CrossFit will be on site this weekend drug testing athletes.

Drug testing at an offseason competition is a notable step. Crash Crucible is a noteable event after the CrossFit Games, and having CrossFit testing on site puts the event in line with the standards athletes face during the official season.

P Score Is Out
Howell also confirmed several changes to the competition format, starting with scoring no longer using P Score. The event is moving to the CrossFit Open format, where athletes earn points based on their placement in each event. The athlete with the lowest point total at the end of the weekend wins.

A Tiebreak That Rewards Consistency
The tiebreak is flipping as well. Most competitions break ties by counting first place finishes. Crash Crucible is doing the opposite and breaking ties by the best worst place finish.

Here is how it works. If Colten Mertens and Sam Demeester finish the weekend tied in points, the tiebreak goes to each athlete's lowest finish of the competition. If Mertens' worst finish is 10th and Demeester's worst finish is 11th, Mertens wins the tiebreak. The rule rewards the athlete who avoids a bad event, not the one who stacks event wins.

Event 4 Farmers Carry Gets a Twist
Athletes also got a first look at all the equipment for each event. For Event 4, the farmers carry will not be a standard kettlebell carry. Rope handles are knotted onto the kettlebell handles, as pictured above, forcing athletes to grip the rope instead of the steel. Expect grip and forearm endurance to be tested in a way a normal carry does not.

About the Event
Crash Crucible is an elite only competition featuring Individual and Masters 35+ divisions, with more than $25,000 in combined prize money on the line. Competition runs Friday, October 9 through Sunday, October 11, with action starting at 9 a.m. ET each day and streaming live on FloElite.

The Action Starts Tomorrow
With new scoring, a new tiebreak, drug testing on site, and a farmers carry nobody has likely been training for, the excitement starts tomorrow, and it is sure to be a fun weekend. Top Story August 17, 2026

Angelo DiCicco Says CrossFit Will Honor The Original Team Prize Purse

The 2026 CrossFit Games team prize purse story is over. Angelo DiCicco said on the Mayhem podcast this morning that in a meeting with CrossFit he was told CrossFit will honor the original prize purse number for the team division.

That closes the loop on a story that has been building for weeks. Teams were shown published payouts that did not match the numbers in the rulebook, and DiCicco now says the athletes are getting the money they were originally promised.

How We Got Here
CrossFit published its official prize purse breakdown after the Games and stated that payouts are based on net revenue from the Open rather than the total number of Open registrations. That moved the total prize purse from the widely expected $2,546,920 down to $2,428,320, which meant the team pool fell from $250,000 to $242,832.

The bigger problem was the placement math. The rulebook example gives the winning team 30 percent of the team pool, but the published first place payout came in at 30.93 percent. Teams finishing 5th through 9th were the ones who got hit, with reported payouts well below the rulebook percentages while everyone else landed slightly above.

The Barbell Spin Would Not Let It Go
The Barbell Spin was persistent in pointing out the mistake. The Spin pundits questioned the numbers right after the podium ceremony, kept at it across multiple articles, broke the math down at length on the Wednesday show, and posted about the topic several times on Instagram. It was the same persistence they showed during the week of the Games. CrossFit made a mistake, and they made sure it did not get quietly buried.

Now DiCicco says CrossFit has committed to the original figures, so the teams who were shorted are made whole. We can finally put that story to bed.

Featured Article July 10, 2026

Your Diet Is a Prison Sentence

Many people treat going on a diet like a prison sentence: X amount of weeks at whatever penitentiary of your choice keto, intermittent fasting, paleo, or any of the other diets you can find on Instagram. Any of the above penitentiaries can leave you with a new outlook on life if you follow the rules, do your allotted sentence, and maybe even get out early for good behavior. For those who have trouble sticking to a diet or fail to see results, it’s easy to get bogged down by following a set of rules and feel trapped within them.

Ultimately, what makes a lasting difference from a diet is what you can learn from it. The things you learn are how the food you eat makes you feel, what is too much food, and what is just not enough.

Food serves two purposes: it provides us with fuel and fulfillment when eaten with friends or family. Both are extremely important. If your diet means you can’t go out to eat with people, then it really is a prison sentence. If your diet means you only eat food from a bag, box, or Styrofoam container, that is an early death sentence.

Ultimately, the foods that are best for us to eat are whole foods. I’m not talking about the overpriced grocery store. I consider whole foods to be foods where the ingredient list is the same as the label. Eating more whole foods will fuel your body and help you feel and perform better.

When you’re out to eat with your friends at the bar and it comes time to order, there are three options: I’m here to enjoy myself, I’m here to enjoy myself while compromising on my goals, or I’m going to stick to my goals.

Option one is easy. It means you order two IPAs, a double cheeseburger, and a side of fries.

Option two is a bit harder, but it allows you to enjoy yourself with some compromise. You order two light beers, a double cheeseburger, and a side salad.

Option three is when you order a club soda with lime, a double cheeseburger without the bun, a side salad, and sweet potato fries.

All three of these options are necessary to fuel us and bring us fulfillment. Finding a balance between these three options is the challenge. An easy way to think about it is this: if you were to eat out six times, three of them should be option three, two of them should be option two, and one of them should be option one.

This would lead to a good balance: half of the time, you’re committed to your goals; a third of the time, you’re making compromises to stay close to your goals while enjoying yourself; and around twenty percent of the time, you give yourself the freedom to enjoy good food in the process.

Consuming a diet of primarily whole foods at the above frequency will be a step in the right direction and will lead to positive changes. Weighing and measuring every morsel will lead to more precise results, but it is difficult to maintain over a long time or when life requires you to eat out. Keeping a food log in the Notes app on your phone or taking pictures of each meal is a great way to track your food, build the habit of pursuing quality food, and better understand the quantity of meals that you’re eating.

With food providing us with fuel and fulfillment, an approach that allows us to focus on eating more whole foods and gives us the flexibility to sustain it for a lifetime seems too good to be true, but it’s not. A diet is a prison sentence you can do for a set period of time and get some results based on the duration and effort you put toward it. If no habits are changed or formed at the end of your sentence, then your results will not last, and you’ll be back for a new sentencing of your choice.

Focusing on eating whole foods most of the time while tracking the quantity and overall quality of your meals will help you break free of this cycle and live a healthier life.

— William Plummer, CFL3
