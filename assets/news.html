Final Recap April 19, 2026

Rodgers and Hoffer Secure Mayhem Classic Crowns

Paige Rodgers and Victor Hoffer won the 2026 Mayhem Classic in Cookeville, Tennessee, after six events across three days. Rodgers used consistent top finishes on the heavier and technical events to beat a women’s field that included Emma Lawson, Lucy Campbell, Gabriela Migala, Abigail Domit, and Olivia Kerstetter. She closed the competition by finishing inside the top three on the final workout of dumbbell bench, double-unders, and a dumbbell farmers lunge to secure the overall title and a 2026 CrossFit Games berth.

On the men’s side, Hoffer climbed steadily up the leaderboard and moved into first overall with an event win on Froning’s Benchmark, a rope climb, handstand walk, and heavy front squat test. He then held his lead through the final workout to finish ahead of Roman Khrennikov and Jeffrey Adler and claim the Mayhem Classic title. Hoffer’s win, along with his consistency across all six events, secured his qualification for the 2026 CrossFit Games.

The final podium featured Rodgers, Lawson, and Campbell for the women, and Hoffer, Khrennikov, and Adler for the men. All six athletes earned direct qualification spots to the 2026 CrossFit Games, with Mayhem Classic serving as the first individual Semifinal of the season and setting the early competitive order heading into the remaining Semifinals.

Day 1 Recap April 18, 2026

Mayhem Classic Day Two Delivers Tight Races And Leaderboard Shifts

Mayhem Classic Day One delivered tight races and a leaderboard shakeup driven by three very different tests, a grip draining barbell benchmark, a sandbag squat grind, and an interval style gymnastics closer.

The second event of the weekend, “Gwen,” turned into a pure grip test on the mens side, with multiple athletes failing reps in the critical set of 9 as barbell cycling under fatigue caught up with them. Calum Clements handled the pacing and breaks best, taking the mens event win in 4:09.14. On the womens side, Abigail Domit claimed the victory with a time of 2:48.25, one of only two women to finish under three minutes on the day.

Event 3 shifted the focus to volume squatting and gymnastics, with athletes tackling 90 total reps of a moderately heavy sandbags as the primary squat test for the weekend. There were no major mishaps on this piece; instead, the difference came down to ring and bar muscle up strategy, how efficiently athletes managed transitions, and how well they maintained their sandbag squat cycle speed as fatigue built up. In the womens field, Lucy Campbell executed best, taking the event win in 10:19.94 and reinforcing her place near the top of the standings. On the mens side, Colten Mertens put on a strong performance, winning in 9:10.66 and continuing to trend upward after a solid start to the day.

The final test of Day One, Event 4, was an interval style workout that rewarded those who could hang on to high volume toes to bar sets in the first two intervals of the workout. The winners in both divisions finished in the third interval. For the men, Roman Khrennikov opening with 49 unbroken toes to bar and then sneaking in one more rep before the first interval ended, establishing a lead he would not relinquish. He went on to win the event in 10:21, capping his day with a strong push that tightened the race for the overall lead. On the womens side, Claudia Gluck took the Event 4 victory in 10:29, to close her day on a high note.

On the womens leaderboard after four events, Paige Rodgers leads with 340 points, followed by Emma Lawson with 310 and Lucinda Campbell with 305, with Gabriela Migala and Emily Rolfe rounding out the top five. On the mens side, Jeffrey Adler sits first overall with 355 points, with Khrennikov close behind at 340 and Mertens and Victor Hoffer tied at 335, ahead of fifth placed Austin Hatfield at 295.

There is still one more day of competition left, with two more workouts to determine who will punch their ticket to this years CrossFit Games.

Event 1 Recap April 17, 2026

Mayhem Classic Opens The 5K Run With A Twist

Event 1 at the 2026 Mayhem Classic was a 5K run with a surprise added on site Friday morning. The workout had already been released as a straight 5K, but Rich Froning revealed to athletes that at some point on the course they would turn onto his private property and complete 50 burpees over a 42 inch log.

The run opened with climbing that included a section at a 17% grade before athletes ever reached the burpee station, forcing them to manage early leg and lung fatigue. The log itself ran at least 80 feet to provide 20 four foot wide lanes, one for each athlete, and was built specifically for this event by Rich’s dad and members of the CrossFit Mayhem crew. After finishing their 50 burpees over the log, athletes headed back onto the course to complete the rest of the 5K under mostly sunny skies and very little shade.

Haley Adams took the win in Event 1, clocking 30:20, with Emily Rolfe finishing 44 seconds behind her. On the men’s side, Austin Hatfield led most of the event before Jeffrey Adler surged past him late to take the win, with Hatfield holding on for second and Saxon Panchik rounding out the top three. With only this single test on Friday, athletes now have just under 24 hours to recover before tackling “Gwen” as Event 2 on Saturday, when the first women’s heat takes the floor at noon and kicks off a three event day.

There was live coverage on CF Network throughout the event with Sevan Matossian and Jenny Hull, with help from people on the ground including coach Taylor Self, Bryson Delmonte, Jonathan Ortega, Melissa @dropin_crossfitgirl on Instgram and special guest Rich Froning himself. Top Story April 16, 2026

Justin Medeiros Withdraws From Legends, Sets Sights on Australia With Ellie Turner

Justin Medeiros has withdrawn from the Legends Championship. During an appearance on Andrew Hiller’s show, at around the 22:18 mark, Brian Friend mentioned that Medeiros and Ellie Turner are planning to compete at the Torian Pro in Australia.

Neither athlete appears to have come through the public Torian qualifying route, so if they are on the roster it is likely via some form of invite, backfill, or discretionary placement rather than the standard qualification.

Ellie Turner last competed in Australia in 2023, when she won the Oceania Semifinal, and she has not returned to the competition floor there since. Since then she has mostly competed in the United States, including events where she ultimately had to withdraw from before finishing out the weekend, so her recent live competition resume is thin despite a solid Open result this season.

Turner placed 38th worldwide in the Open but then struggled badly in Quarterfinals, finishing 696th. She did not complete the Torian Pro qualifier and, based on the available information, would almost certainly not be in line for a Torian Pro spot if it were not for Justin Medeiros reaching out to ask for the invite and the event extending a corresponding opportunity to her as well.

The real question now is how the Australian fans back home will feel about seeing her return to the Torian floor under these circumstances.

Source: Legends Championship update, Brian Friend on Andrew Hiller YouTube show (~22:18) , Torian Pro qualification info

Injury Update April 15, 2026

Jayson Hopper Withdraws From Mayhem Classic Due to Groin Injury

Jayson Hopper has withdrawn from the Mayhem Classic due to a groin injury. The Mayhem Classic posted to their story his name card and custom recovery mat for the event suggesting he is no longer competing this weekend. CF Network News has confirmed that he is still not healed from the groin injury that Hopper shared on his Instagram story, and at this time it is unclear whether he will be healthy in time to compete at any in person semifinals this season.

Given the nature of the injury and the current timeline, the safest option for Hopper is to prioritize recovery and target the online semifinals beginning June 11th. Focusing on healing rather than rushing back to the competition floor should give him the best chance at qualifying for the CrossFit Games to defend his title.

We will keep you updated as more information about his recovery and competition plans becomes available.

Source: Mayhem Classic Instagram story, CF Network News
