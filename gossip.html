Family News

Ricky Garard Is Going To Be A Dad

Ricky Garard and his wife Michelle are expecting their first child together. The Australian CrossFit star shared the news on Instagram with a series of photos, captioning the post, “Our greatest adventure yet, baby Garard,” and saying the couple is counting down the days until they meet their little one.

Garard and Michelle were married earlier this year, making this a huge year of life updates for the couple. From a wedding in February to a baby announcement just months later, the Garards are clearly having a very big 2026 away from the competition floor.

Source: Ricky Garard Instagram announcement.

Event Gossip

Wodapalooza SoCal Teases Battle Of The Camps

Gymreapers Wodapalooza SoCal announced on Instagram that this year’s format will be different, teasing a Battle Of The Camps showdown in Huntington Beach from September 25 to 27 with more details still to come. The only real question now is who will take it, PRVN, Brute, or Mayhem, or will there be a team of people who don't belong to any camp?

Camp Drama

Brute Strength Just Took A Shot At Every Major Camp

Brute Strength took the top two spots on both the men’s and women’s side at the 2026 CrossFit Games, with James Sprague and Dallin Pepper going one two for the men and Aimee Cringle and Emma Lawson doing the same on the women’s side.

Shortly after the podium ceremony, Brute posted to Instagram with the caption, “It’s a PRVN fact we came to cause some Mayhem. A good reminder Hard Work Pays Off...” which looked like a pretty direct shot at the other major camps in the space by name. When you reference PRVN, Mayhem, and HWPO all in one victory lap, people are going to read it as more than just a celebration post.

The thing is, the flex lands because the results back it up. With how brutal this year’s Games looked from start to finish, there is no real argument that those podium spots were handed to anyone, Brute’s athletes earned every bit of it.

Source: Brute Strength Instagram and 2026 CrossFit Games results posts.
