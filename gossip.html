Comeback Twist

Mat Fraser's 2027 Comeback Is Not A CrossFit Games Run

Mat Fraser is competing again in 2027, just not in a CrossFit Games season. Speaking to Men's Health UK at the Rogue Invitational in Aberdeen, the five time Games champion said he is training for an ultra endurance race he expects could take 24 to 30 hours. He did not name the event, and he joked that his videographer posted the comeback tease "just to get the chatter going."

Fans hoping for a Masters run can stop waiting. Asked if he was considering it, Fraser answered, "No... the interest level of doing that is way too low." His recent Grand Canyon rim to rim to rim trip, roughly 45 miles on foot, helped open the door to endurance, even though he admitted, "I got slowly conned into it."

He has not hidden how big this one feels. "Doing something like a 24 hour race, it scares the sh*t out of me," Fraser said, adding that becoming a dad pushed him to ask, "Is this what I want my final form to be?" His answer: "I'm not in my final form. I'm not ready to get a La Z Boy and kick my feet up."

Source: Men's Health UK interview at the Rogue Invitational, October 2026.

Calendar Shakeup

No More REBEL Renegade Games In 2027

The REBEL Renegade Games are off the 2027 CrossFit season calendar. "After four incredible seasons, the REBEL Renegade Games will no longer form part of the CrossFit season calendar. There will be no REBEL Renegade Games in 2027," organizers announced on Instagram.

It comes down to qualifying spots. "Without qualifying spots and the CrossFit Semifinal designation, we cannot deliver the event our sponsors and community deserve," the post reads. The event has been the in person CrossFit Games qualifier for athletes across Africa and the UAE, and this past May in Johannesburg it sent one man, one woman, and one team to the Games.

Organizers say they are not going anywhere. "This one hurts, but the mission hasn't changed. We're still here, and we're still growing CrossFit across Africa and the UAE."

Source: REBEL Renegade Games (@rebelrenegadegames) Instagram post, October 2026.

Camp Shakeup

Roman Khrennikov Is No Longer A Mayhem Athlete

Roman Khrennikov has officially left Mayhem. "I want to let you know that I'm no longer a Mayhem athlete," he shared on Instagram, adding, "I feel that this chapter has come to a natural close, and now it's time for me to build something of my own." He has not said what that next chapter will look like.

The announcement comes just days after Roman told the Out of the Cage podcast that he is moving his family from Cookeville, Tennessee, back to Madison, Wisconsin, saying the city is a better fit for his son's education. Madison is where he lived in 2022 after finally getting his visa, before he moved to Cookeville that December to train at CrossFit Mayhem.

Roman is at least the third big name to leave Mayhem this year. Haley Adams moved home and now works with Coach El at Brute, and Austin Hatfield is now with PRVN. Three exits in one off season has the community wondering who is next.

Source: Roman Khrennikov (@roman_khrennikov) Instagram post and Out of the Cage podcast, October 2026.
