Performance Buzz

Enhanced Games Debuts in Las Vegas

The Enhanced Games hit Las Vegas last night with swimming, weightlifting, and track events built entirely around the idea of performance without traditional anti doping rules. With steroids, peptides, and other banned substances openly allowed, the whole pitch is about chasing “peak human performance” in the most literal way possible and seeing how far enhanced athletes can actually push the record books. The debut has already lit up the sports world, with some calling it a futuristic science experiment and others wondering where the line is between sport and a pharmacology showcase.

Competition Buzz

Grace Walton Withdraws From Torian Pro

During the Torian Pro broadcast it was announced that Grace Walton had withdrawn from the competition due to medical reasons, cutting her weekend short just as things were heating up. After the news broke, Grace took to her Instagram story to thank everyone for the messages and support, and she kept it positive by saying she’ll be back soon, hinting that this is just a pause and not the end of her season.

HQ Shake-Up

New Chief People Officer Back At CrossFit HQ

Bruce Edwards’ first big move as CrossFit’s new CEO is bringing back a familiar face, with Kyla Pavlina returning to the company as Chief People Officer. Pavlina previously served as CrossFit’s Head of Human Resources from 2013 to 2019, overlapping with Edwards’ last run as COO, and she steps into the role previously held by Annette Reavis. She’s now back in the building and already has the community wondering what changes might be coming for staff, affiliates, and the overall culture at CrossFit HQ.
