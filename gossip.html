Injury Watch

Justin Medeiros Is Out After An Accident

Justin Medeiros announced on Instagram that he is out following an accident. He did not share details about what happened or the extent of the injury, leaving fans waiting for more information on his recovery and when he may be able to compete again.

The announcement creates an immediate question around Ellie Turner, who appears to still be expected at the event. Nothing has been confirmed about whether her plans have changed, but with Justin now out, fans will be watching closely to see if Ellie still makes the trip and takes the floor.

Source: Justin Medeiros Instagram announcement.

Media Drama

Sevan And Hiller Make Up

Sevan Matossian and Andrew Hiller appear to have made up after their unexpected falling out and are now working together at Wodapalooza SoCal. The reunion comes just as the new Battle Of The Camps division arrives in Huntington Beach, where the competition runs September 25 through 27.

The partnership seems to have left a couple of people who exited the chat feeling some type of way, with Tyler Watkins in particular posting obscure conspiracy style reels that appear to need a Tyler translator before anyone can fully decode them.

So who benefits from the partnerships? Everyone does, until they do not.

Source: Wodapalooza SoCal media coverage, social media posts, and community chatter.

Family News

Ricky Garard Is Having A Boy

Ricky Garard and wife Michelle have revealed that their first child is a boy. The couple shared the gender reveal on Instagram, with Ricky calling the moment intense and saying they cannot wait to meet Baby G.

The update comes just weeks after the Garards announced they were expecting their first child, meaning the CrossFit world now has another future athlete to keep an eye on. Baby Garard is already getting a very warm welcome from fans, and the family news keeps rolling for one of Australia’s biggest names in the sport.

Source: Ricky Garard Instagram gender reveal post.
