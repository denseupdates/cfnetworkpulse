Workout of the Week

Week of July 13, 2026 2026 CrossFit Games · Individual Event 1: The 2007 Hopper

Warm-up

General warm-up — 2 rounds at an easy pace: 250 m row 10 shoulder pass-throughs with a PVC 10 band pull-aparts 10 scap pull-ups 10 air squats 5 inchworms with a push-up Then… Shoulder and pulling prep — 2 sets: 5 kip swings 5 kipping pull-ups 5 strict presses (empty bar) 5 push presses 5 push jerks Then… Build to your workout weight over 3–5 sets of 3 push jerks (touch-and-go from the floor). Finish with 2 short sets of 5 pulls on the rower + 4 pull-ups + 3 push jerks at workout weight.

Cool-down

1 minute doorway pec stretch, each side 1 minute lat stretch on the pull-up bar, each side 1 minute banded shoulder stretch, each side 1 minute forearm / wrist stretch, each side 2 minute couch stretch, each side 1 minute pigeon stretch, each side 1 minute seated forward fold

Scaling Notes

  • Rx: 1,000 m row, then 5 rounds of 25 pull-ups and 7 push jerks at 135 / 85 lb.
  • Intermediate: 5 rounds of 15 pull-ups and 7 push jerks at 115 / 75 lb.
  • Beginner: 5 rounds of 15 jumping pull-ups (or banded / ring rows) and 7 push jerks at 95 / 65 lb.
  • Stimulus: A grip- and shoulder-endurance test. Row steady in 3:30–5:00, break the pull-ups into 3–5 sets, and keep the push jerks unbroken. Target time is 12–17 minutes.
  • Score: Post your total time from the start of the row to the last push jerk in the comments below.
